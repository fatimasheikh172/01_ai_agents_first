SYNC vs ASYNC - Easy Explanation

SYNC (Synchronous):
- Kaam ek ke baad ek hota hai.
- Har step dusre step ka intezaar karta hai.
- Example: Tum pehle chai banate ho, phir uske baad anda fry karte ho.

ASYNC (Asynchronous):
- Kaam ek sath chal sakta hai.
- Ek kaam jab wait kar raha hota hai (e.g. chai ban rahi hai), tum dusra kaam start kar sakte ho (e.g. anda fry).
- Program rukta nahi, dusra kaam karta rehta hai.

Python Example:

SYNC:
    print("Start task 1")
    time.sleep(3)  # Wait 3 seconds
    print("Task 1 done")

ASYNC:
    async def task():
        await asyncio.sleep(3)
        print("Task done")

Summary:
- SYNC: "Ruko, kaam complete hone do, phir agla karo."
- ASYNC: "Kaam chalu rakho, wait karte hue dusra kaam karo."
